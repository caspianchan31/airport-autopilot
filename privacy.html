Privacy Policy

Airport Autopilot for iPhone and iPad · Effective 2026-09-27

In short
  • The app does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your saved game and settings stay on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • The AI manager runs on your device. The app sends nothing to a cloud AI.

1. Who we are

Airport Autopilot (“the app”) is made by Caspian, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at caspianchan3@gmail.com.

2. What the app does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, from us or from anyone else.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it does not send notifications.
  • We do not run a server for the app. So we do not receive or keep any information about you or about how you play.

3. What stays on your device

The app keeps the following on your device only. It never sends them to us or to anyone else:

  • your saved game (your airport's progress);
  • your settings, such as language, sound volume and AI-manager options;
  • a note of which of our in-app purchases your Apple Account owns, copied from Apple's answer, so the right levels open even when you are offline;
  • a technical log of game events (for example flights handled, weather reports and purchase status). It is kept only on the device and is limited in size.

Your saved game and settings can be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. Settings → Start over resets your airport's progress.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. The app uses Apple's StoreKit to check which of our items your Apple Account owns, so it can unlock them and restore them. Apple gives developers aggregated reports (such as numbers of sales and downloads) that do not identify individual players. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. The AI manager

The in-game AI manager is a set of rules built into the app. It works on every device, also offline. On supported devices with iOS 26 or later and Apple Intelligence turned on, the app also uses Apple's on-device language model (the Foundation Models framework) to rank options and to write short in-game comments. The model receives only game information, such as flights, stands and ground crews. It never receives personal information, and it runs on your device. The app does not send game data or personal data to any cloud AI service.

6. Radio voices

The tower radio is spoken by your device's built-in speech synthesis. The app does not use the microphone.

7. Network connections

The app connects to Apple's App Store (StoreKit) to show prices, make purchases and restore them.

To show Innsbruck's real weather, the app also downloads the latest public weather report (METAR) for Innsbruck Airport (LOWI) from aviationweather.gov, a service of the U.S. National Weather Service (NOAA). It does this when a game starts and every 30 minutes while you play. The request (https://aviationweather.gov/api/data/metar?ids=LOWI&format=json) asks only for Innsbruck's report and identifies the app only by the fixed name “ai-airport-game/0.1”, the same for every player. It contains no personal data, no device or user identifiers and nothing about your game. Like any internet request, it shows your device's IP address to that service, which handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not receive it. When your device is offline, the game uses default weather.

8. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your choices

Because the app does not collect personal data, we hold no such data about you to show, correct or delete. You can remove everything the app stored on your device by deleting the app. For information Apple holds about your purchases, please contact Apple.

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to answer you, we do not share them, and we delete them when they are no longer needed for your request (at the latest after 12 months). You can ask us to delete them sooner. If you live in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They use no cookies, no analytics and no content from other websites. The host records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes (GitHub Pages documentation).

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a future version of the app ever collects data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before that version is released.

12. Contact

Caspian · caspianchan3@gmail.com

隐私政策

机场搭子（Airport Autopilot），适用于 iPhone 和 iPad · 生效日期 2026-09-27

简单说
  • 本 App 不收集个人数据：没有账号，没有广告，没有统计，不做追踪。
  • 存档和设置只留在你的设备上。
  • 购买由 Apple 处理，我们看不到你的付款信息。
  • AI 经理在你的设备上运行，App 不把任何东西发给云端 AI。

1. 我们是谁

机场搭子（下称“本 App”）由独立开发者 Caspian（下称“我们”）制作。联系邮箱：caspianchan3@gmail.com。

2. 本 App 不做的事

  • 不需要注册账号，也不需要登录。
  • 不含任何统计、崩溃上报、广告或追踪软件，我们自己的和第三方的都没有。
  • 不申请定位、通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风权限，也不发通知。
  • 我们没有为本 App 架设服务器，所以收不到、也不保存任何关于你或你怎么玩的信息。

3. 留在设备上的内容

以下内容只存在你的设备上，本 App 不会发给我们或任何人：

  • 游戏存档（机场进度）；
  • 设置，例如语言、音量和 AI 经理的选项；
  • 你的 Apple 账户买过哪些内购项目（照抄 Apple 给的结果），这样离线时也能打开对应的等级；
  • 一份游戏事件的技术日志（例如处理过的航班、天气报文、购买状态），只存在本机，大小有上限。

存档和设置可能会随设备的 iCloud 或电脑备份一起备份，这些备份由你和 Apple 管理。删除 App 就会删除它存的全部内容；在「设置 → 重新开始」可以清空当前机场的进度。

4. App 内购买

购买通过 Apple 的 App Store 完成，付款由 Apple 处理。我们拿不到你的姓名、付款信息或 Apple 账户。本 App 用 Apple 的 StoreKit 查询你的 Apple 账户拥有哪些我们的商品，以便解锁和恢复购买。Apple 给开发者的是汇总报表（例如销量、下载量），无法识别到个人。Apple 如何处理你的信息，请看 Apple 隐私政策。

5. AI 经理

游戏里的 AI 经理是内置在 App 里的一套规则，所有设备都能用，离线也能用。在装有 iOS 26 或更新版本、并已打开 Apple 智能的受支持设备上，本 App 还会调用 Apple 的设备端语言模型（Foundation Models 框架），用来给候选操作排序、写几句游戏内的点评。模型只拿到游戏信息（航班、机位、地勤等），从不拿到个人信息，而且在你的设备上运行。本 App 不会把游戏数据或个人数据发给任何云端 AI 服务。

6. 无线电语音

塔台无线电由设备自带的语音合成朗读。本 App 不使用麦克风。

7. 联网

本 App 会连接 Apple 的 App Store（StoreKit），用来显示价格、购买和恢复购买。

为了显示因斯布鲁克的真实天气，本 App 还会从美国国家海洋和大气管理局（NOAA）下属国家气象局（NWS）的 aviationweather.gov 下载因斯布鲁克机场（LOWI）最新的公开天气报文（METAR）：开局时一次，游戏进行中每 30 分钟一次。请求（https://aviationweather.gov/api/data/metar?ids=LOWI&format=json）只查询因斯布鲁克的报文，只用固定名称“ai-airport-game/0.1”标明来自本 App（所有玩家都一样）；其中没有个人数据、没有设备或用户标识，也没有你的游戏内容。和所有联网请求一样，对方会看到你设备的 IP 地址，并按它自己的隐私政策处理；我们收不到。设备离线时，游戏使用默认天气。

8. 儿童

本 App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

9. 你的选择

本 App 不收集个人数据，所以我们手里没有关于你的这类数据可供查看、更正或删除。删除 App 即可清除它在设备上存的全部内容。Apple 保存的购买记录，请联系 Apple。

如果你给我们发邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和邮件内容，只用来回复你，不给任何人；处理完不再需要时删除（最迟 12 个月后）。你也可以要求我们提前删除。如果你在欧盟、欧洲经济区或英国，也可以向当地的数据保护机构投诉。

10. 本网站

这些页面托管在 GitHub Pages。页面不用 Cookie、不做统计、不加载其他网站的内容。托管方出于安全目的会记录访问者的 IP 地址（见 GitHub Pages 说明）。

11. 变更

本政策如有变更，我们会更新本页和顶部日期。如果将来某个版本开始收集数据，我们会在该版本发布之前更新本政策和 App Store 上的隐私信息。
